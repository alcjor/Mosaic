2 Telecommands

Each telecommand is listed below with its service, its APID and its arguments. Positions count from the start of the application data field.

2.1 PWRC0101 PWR_HTR_A_ON

PUS (8,1), APID 0x101. Switch battery heater A on.

PositionArgumentTypeRangeDescription
1PWRP00013/41 to 2Heater control mode: 1 thermostat, 2 forced on

2.2 PWRC0112 PWR_HTR_A_OFF

PUS (8,1), APID 0x101. Switch battery heater A off.

No arguments.

2.3 PWRC0123 PWR_SET_BAT_EOC

PUS (8,1), APID 0x101. Set the battery end-of-charge voltage.

Critical command: it needs an arm and fire sequence.

PositionArgumentTypeRangeDescription
1PWRP00105/130.0 to 33.6 VEnd-of-charge voltage, 30.0 to 33.6 V
