# StepRow

A 30px ShardBadge beside a `step-title` and a one- or two-line `body-print` description: the unit of "How it works".

- The canonical four steps are Tesserae → Pattern → Build → Embed. Keep these names; adjust descriptions to the audience.
- Step 2 must always say who decides: "Machines propose, people decide."
- Badges in `heading` with the number in `surface`, so they hold on paper and on navy.
- Gap between badge and text `space-2` + 2px; between rows `space-3` − 2px (10px).
- To show the build as pictures rather than words, use the BuildStrip.
