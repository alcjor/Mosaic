# ShardStrip

A full-bleed band of small blue shards on white grout that separates a navy header from a white body, like the edge of a trencadís bench.

- Use the `flyer-strip` image (Imagery group) at 14px tall on A4, 20px on 16:9 slides; always edge to edge.
- At most two per page: under the header, and optionally above the footer.
- Decorative: empty `alt` and `aria-hidden="true"`.
- To change colours, regenerate it with the brand script; never tint the image with CSS filters.
