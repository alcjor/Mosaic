# CheckPanel

A `surface-tint` panel with a `panel-title`, three or four check-marked claims and one closing `caption`: the place for the trust argument.

- Background `surface-tint`, radius `radius-md`, padding `space-3` + 2px / `space-4`.
- Title "What stays true". Claims come from the six rules: a bold lead of two to five words ("No re-entry:") and a short fact.
- The closing caption is the trust line, verbatim: "Machines propose, people decide."
- One per piece. It is the only filled box on a page; everything else sits on paper.
