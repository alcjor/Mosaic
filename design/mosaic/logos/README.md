# Logos

"The last shard": a square tile of three shards with a fourth, lighter shard being slotted into its corner, alone or locked up with the wordmark.

- `mosaic-lockup-light`: on white. Mark in `brand-navy` with an `accent` shard; wordmark `brand-navy`, category line `accent`.
- `mosaic-lockup-dark`: on `brand-navy`. Mark in white and `shard-tcs` with a `shard-eps` shard; wordmark white, category line `shard-tcs`.
- `mosaic-mark-light`, `mosaic-mark-dark`: the mark alone, transparent background, for light and navy grounds.
- `mosaic-app-icon-512`, `-180`, `-32`: the dark mark on a solid navy square, for app icons, favicons and avatars.
- SVGs (text converted to outlines) for documents and slides; PNG where SVG isn't accepted.
- Clear space: a quarter of the mark's width. Minimum: 16px / 8 mm (mark), 120px / 35 mm (lockup).
- The wordmark is set in the stand-in face (DejaVu Sans); regenerate the lockups with `generate_logo.py` once the corporate typeface is confirmed.

## MOSAIC Tessera (sub-brand)

"Joined": one tile in two colours, ours (navy) and the customer’s (turquoise), meeting along a clean, shard-like joint. It marks Tessera, the connected data pack we deliver to customers, apart from the MOSAIC app.

- `tessera-lockup-light`: on white. Mark in `brand-navy` and `tessera`; “MOSAIC” in `brand-navy`, “Tessera” in `tessera`, descriptor “The connected data pack” in `ink-muted`.
- `tessera-lockup-dark`: on `brand-navy`. Mark in `tessera-ours-bright` and `tessera-bright`; “MOSAIC” white, “Tessera” `tessera-bright`, descriptor `shard-tcs`.
- `tessera-mark-light`, `tessera-mark-dark`: the mark alone, transparent background.
- `tessera-app-icon-512`, `-180`, `-32`: the dark mark on a solid navy square.
- Clear space and minimum sizes as for the MOSAIC mark. Generated by `generate_tessera.py`.
